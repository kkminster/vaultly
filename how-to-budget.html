How to Budget Monthly

Budgeting helps you take control of your money instead of wondering where it goes. A simple monthly plan can reduce stress and help you save more consistently.

1. Know your income

Start with your total monthly income after tax. This is the amount you actually have available to spend.

2. List your essential costs

These include rent, bills, food, transport, and anything you must pay each month.

3. Separate needs and wants

Needs are essential. Wants are optional. This helps you control overspending without cutting everything.

4. Set a savings amount

Treat savings like a bill you must pay. Even small amounts add up over time.

5. Track your spending

Use a tool like Vaultly to see where your money is going and adjust your budget if needed.

6. Adjust each month

Your budget should change as your situation changes. Review it regularly and improve it over time.

Final thought

Budgeting is not about restriction. It is about control and making your money work for you.

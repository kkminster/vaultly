How to Build an Emergency Fund

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected expenses like repairs, bills, or sudden loss of income. It helps you avoid debt and stay financially stable.

1. Start small

You do not need thousands immediately. Even £100–£500 is a strong starting point.

2. Aim for 3–6 months of expenses

A full emergency fund should cover several months of essential costs like rent, food, and bills.

3. Save regularly

Set a weekly or monthly amount and treat it like a fixed bill.

4. Keep it separate

Store your emergency fund in a separate account so you are less tempted to spend it.

5. Use it only for emergencies

This money is for real emergencies only, not everyday spending.

Final thought

Building an emergency fund takes time, but it gives you financial security and peace of mind.
